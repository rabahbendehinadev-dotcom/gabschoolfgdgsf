---
name: Solutions public privacy
description: Durable privacy boundary between public solution discovery and protected technical content.
---

Public and locked Solutions surfaces must use a strict server-side allowlist containing only brand, model, sanitized problem category, generic public title/excerpt, publication date, safe slug, and a privacy-safe AI cover. Tool, software, method, mode, procedures, screenshots, resources, prerequisites, results, internal tags, and raw technical titles/excerpts must remain available only to active VIP users and authorized admins.

**Why:** Protected technical metadata previously leaked through cards, search/filter taxonomies, slugs, cover labels, and publication notifications even when article content was locked.

**How to apply:** Derive and persist separate public metadata from brand/model/problem; use it for public responses, search, filters, notifications, links, and AI cover prompts. Public covers must never use custom uploads or article screenshots. Preserve old links through slug history and permanent redirects.
